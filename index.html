Our mission

Wellness Tech LLC is a Virginia LLC, formed in 2020. We make patient-education and clinician apps for kidney health. The apps work offline, with no login, no ads, and no data collection.

The people behind it

Founders

Amar K. Damodar, MD, FASN

Co-founder

Amar K. Damodar, MD, FASN, is a board-certified nephrologist with Commonwealth Kidney Doctors in the Richmond, Virginia area. He has practiced nephrology since 2009, and before joining Commonwealth Kidney Doctors in 2023 he spent more than a decade with Southside Kidney Specialists, where he served as a dialysis medical director. He trained in nephrology at the State University of New York at Buffalo, is a Fellow of the American Society of Nephrology and the American College of Physicians, and is a clinical assistant professor at Liberty University College of Osteopathic Medicine. He also developed K Swipe, a free iPhone app that teaches people the potassium content of foods.
